
# 4DSurf: High-Fidelity Dynamic Scene Surface Reconstruction

[![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2603.28064-b31b1b.svg)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28064)
[![CVPR 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/CVPR-2026-blue.svg)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wu_4DSurf_High-Fidelity_Dynamic_Scene_Surface_Reconstruction_CVPR_2026_paper.html)

Official implementation of **4DSurf: High-Fidelity Dynamic Scene Surface Reconstruction**, published at **CVPR 2026**.


## 📦 Reconstructed Meshes

We release the reconstructed meshes generated by **4DSurf** on the following datasets.

| Dataset | Download | Status |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| CMU Panoptic Studio | [GitHub Release](https://github.com/RyansLearning/4DSurf/releases/tag/v1.0-ours-cmu-panoptic-meshes) | ✅ Available |
| Hi4D | [GitHub Release](https://github.com/RyansLearning/4DSurf/releases/tag/v1.0-ours-hi4d-meshes) | ✅ Available |

The reconstructed meshes are organized by scene and distributed as separate downloadable archives.

### Dataset Attribution & Usage

The reconstructed meshes are derived from the original datasets. We gratefully acknowledge the dataset creators for making their data available to the research community.

Please refer to the original dataset websites for their respective licenses, terms of use, and citation requirements:

- **[CMU Panoptic Studio](http://domedb.perception.cs.cmu.edu/)**
- **[Hi4D](https://yifeiyin04.github.io/Hi4D/)**

> **Note:** The reconstructed meshes are shared for academic research purposes only. Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with the original datasets' applicable licenses, terms of use, and redistribution restrictions.
